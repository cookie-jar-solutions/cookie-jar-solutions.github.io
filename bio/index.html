CEO and Founder, Cookie Jar Solutions

Anthony Saieva is the CEO and founder of Cookie Jar Solutions, where he advises organizations on AI strategy and adoption, helping leaders translate emerging AI capabilities into measurable enterprise value.

Anthony worked at Millennium across the Equities AI and Equities Management teams, where he helped shape AI strategy and built technology to support the investment process. Previously, he was a researcher at IBM Research as part of the AI Supply Chain Security group.

Anthony holds a PhD from Columbia University, where his research focused on secure systems design and artificial intelligence, including work conducted through research programs supported by DARPA and the National Science Foundation.

His career spans AI research, investment management, and enterprise strategy, with a focus on applying advanced technology to complex, high value business problems.
