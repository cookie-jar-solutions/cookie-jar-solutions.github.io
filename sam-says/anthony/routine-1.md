# Weekly Workout Split (Phase I)

## Notes

- **Cardio:** 15 minutes at low intensity, followed by 15 minutes at mid-to-high intensity (30 minutes total). Adjust the activity to how your knee feels.
- Upper-body exercises are organized by muscle group.
- Keep the daily stretches and the flexibility/mobility routine.

## Stretching & Mobility

**Every workout day:** stretch as part of your warm-up.

**10–15 minutes of flexibility/mobility:**

- Knee hugs / beetles
- Archers
- Split lunges
- T bridges
- 3–4 [sun salutations](https://www.google.com/search?q=sun+salutation&udm=2)

## Cardio Set

1. **Warm-up:** daily stretches and 10–15 minutes of flexibility/mobility (see above).
2. **Main cardio — 30 minutes total:**
   - 15 minutes **low intensity**.
   - 15 minutes **mid-to-high intensity**.
   - Choose the activity based on how your knee feels. Options from the original plan:
     - High-incline treadmill: incline **10**, speed **3.0**.
     - StairMaster: levels **4–6**, changing every **2 minutes**.
3. **Cooldown:** treadmill walk for **5–10 minutes**.

*The original cardio instructions used 15-minute treadmill and StairMaster blocks. The updated intensity split above supersedes those blocks; the equipment settings are retained as reference options, not prescribed intensities.*

## Supplemental Exercise Lists

These are the additional routines from the original notes, kept separate from the weekly schedule.

### Upper-Body Workout

**3 sets of 10 reps**

#### Chest
- Cable chest fly
- Decline chest press
- Incline chest press

#### Back
- Mid/high row (motorcycle grip to ski grip)

#### Shoulders / Upper Back
- Rope face pulls
- X backs

#### Triceps
- Triceps extensions (overhead and straight down)

#### Biceps
- Biceps curls (dumbbells or cable bar)

### Ab Circuit

**3 circuit sets; aim for 10 reps per exercise**, except the timed plank.

- Kneeling VIPR twists
- VIPR around the world
- Ankle touches
- Plank (30 / 45 / 60 seconds)

### Leg Workout

**Only do this if cardio felt okay the day before.**

**4 sets of 12 reps**

- Hack squat press (start with 25-lb plates on each side; increase if necessary)
- Goblet squats (start light and increase weight if needed)
- Bulgarian split squats
- Romanian deadlifts (RDLs)
- Seated leg extensions
